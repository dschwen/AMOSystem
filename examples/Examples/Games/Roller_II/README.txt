Roller II
=========

A puzzle game written in AMOS by Daniel Schwen, 1991-1993 (program and graphics).
Load Roller_II.AMOS in the editor and run it, or start it on its own:
    amos AMOSPro_Examples:Games/Roller_II/Roller_II.AMOS

The game: steer the ball over the board, collect the tiles before the time runs out.
HELP (on the title screen) explains the tiles; EDIT is the level editor.

The program came from its own disk, the volume RII:. Here it finds its files in
AMOSPro_Examples:Games/Roller_II/ instead: only those names were changed (in
Roller_II.AMOS, RollerIntro.amos and EditHELP.amos). The sources also have two later
fixes: the board's edges are checked before the next cell is read (it read past the
level's bank at the bottom right), and the last help page is placed right.
The high scores are saved in this folder (Highscores).
